Scientific interpretation

The run demonstrates that the pipeline can continuously decode and train on a very large set of public NASA GIBS MODIS/VIIRS true-colour geospatial/time windows while maintaining low transport failure rates. The reconstruction objective improved substantially. It does not by itself establish water loss, glacier retreat, drought causation, blocked channels or any other environmental event, because scene-level environmental indices were not persisted during this run.
