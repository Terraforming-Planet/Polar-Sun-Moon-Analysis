Next scientific validation

The next stage is held-out validation on provenance-complete, season-matched satellite scene pairs. Water area, shoreline movement, exposed sediment, river width/centerline and other deterministic measurements should be evaluated independently of the self-supervised training objective. Causal labels such as a blocked inlet/outlet require independent hydrological evidence.

Publication gate: this page reports training metrics only. Environmental findings should be promoted separately after scene-level quality control, reproducible measurements, provenance and uncertainty checks.
