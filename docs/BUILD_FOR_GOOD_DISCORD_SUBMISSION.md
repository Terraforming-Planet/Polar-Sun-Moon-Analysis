## Short description

**Terra Observation System** is an open-source Earth-observation platform built to help communities, researchers, students, NGOs and environmental teams investigate changes in water, rivers, lakes, wetlands, drylands and environmental hazards using legal, official and publicly available data.

The application combines an interactive 3D Earth with NASA, NASA GIBS, ESA/Copernicus/CDSE, USGS, NOAA and other public scientific sources. Users can search a real place, inspect dated satellite imagery, compare environmental evidence, use terrain/DEM and hydrology tools, and ask an OpenAI research assistant to explain what is visible, what remains uncertain, and what should be checked next.

OpenAI is used as an **evidence-grounded explanation and research layer**. It does not manufacture satellite measurements or turn visual patterns into unsupported causes. The project preserves provenance, evidence classes and scientific limitations.

A major long-term goal is to help identify and investigate water loss, changing river/lake systems, damaged or disconnected water pathways, dryland/paleochannel patterns and environmental risk indicators earlier — so people have better evidence for restoration research, preparedness and protection of life, homes, farms, infrastructure and water resources.

Codex was used throughout development for repository audits, architecture, refactoring, tests, CI, GPU research workflows, Earth-observation integrations, 3D interface fixes, scientific guardrails and final BUILD FOR GOOD hardening.

The repository also includes published NVIDIA L4 research, including a NASA GIBS streaming run that processed **200,016 geospatial/time windows across 75 research regions**. These are training/evaluation facts, not environmental ground truth.

**Who it helps:** local communities, farmers/water managers, educators, students, researchers, NGOs, environmental teams and open-source developers.

**How it is used:** search an area → retrieve official/public evidence → inspect satellite/terrain context → compare observations → ask OpenAI to explain findings and uncertainty → save reproducible evidence and recommended next checks.
