SCIENTIFIC CURIOSITIES

What this station helps explain

≈25 days

Solar equator

The Sun is plasma, so it rotates differentially. Equatorial regions rotate faster than high solar latitudes.

≈35 days

High solar latitudes

Near the polar regions the apparent rotation period is substantially longer than at the equator.

Artificial eclipse

LASCO coronagraph

Blocking the bright photosphere reveals the faint corona and helps observers notice comets approaching the Sun.

Dynamic distance

Earth–Moon scale

The Moon’s distance changes continuously. This station calculates the distance from the timestamped JPL vectors stored by the project rather than using one permanent value.

This is the deterministic research index. Every cell keeps a stable CELL-001…CELL-512 identifier and X/Y/Z = 0…7. It can later point to a data tile, LOD sector, point-cloud block, atmosphere sector, orbital observation or planetary analysis region.
