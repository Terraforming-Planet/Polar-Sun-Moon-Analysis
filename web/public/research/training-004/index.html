Training objective and data representation

The objective is masked eight-channel before/after reconstruction plus a derived change head. Each example uses four surface-reflectance channels from the earlier observation and four from the later observation. Source shapes are normalized to a 256 px scientific AOI tensor. Mixed precision was enabled.

Critical interpretation rule

1,223,808 training exposures are not 1,223,808 independent Earth observations. They are repeated and augmented optimization exposures over 95 real scientific temporal pairs. These training metrics describe optimization behaviour; by themselves they do not establish a causal environmental finding or authorize a real-world intervention.

What this experiment contributes

Training #4 demonstrates a concrete EO training workflow on an NVIDIA L4 using real temporal satellite pairs, while the recovery audit also exposed an important reproducibility gap: full continuous raw console logging was not preserved for the target sessions. The project therefore treats logging/evidence initialization as a hard precondition for future long GPU runs. The model is intended to support qualified researchers, communities and relevant services with traceable information; real-world decisions remain with people and applicable scientific, legal and environmental review.
